Choose your input

Pinyin, Ziranma, Wubi 86 and English, with Simplified/Traditional output and a remembered scheme.

Slide together

Combine each thumb’s starting key and endpoint. Legal combinations stay clear; both thumbs release to enter once.

Compose in Buffer

Edit locally, then tap to insert one block or hold for one second to insert the remaining text.

Optional translation

Apple on-device translation uses supported languages and downloaded models on iOS 26 or later. Optional AI uses your own HTTPS provider and API key, after you consent to that provider.

iPhone · iOS 17+ for ordinary typing and Buffer · No app account, ads or keyboard-text telemetry. External AI requires Full Access. Availability of Apple translation depends on your system and language models.
